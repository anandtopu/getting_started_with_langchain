# Code of conduct

This is a learning community. Welcome questions, explain disagreements with evidence, and make room for people with different experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not a person's ability or identity. Use respectful language and give people a chance to correct mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not include real customer information or credentials in examples, discussions or issue reports.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, request corrections, limit participation or block repeated harmful behavior. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected participants. Report concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact route on their profile; do not post identifying details publicly. Maintainers should disclose conflicts of interest and seek an uninvolved reviewer when necessary.

These expectations apply to repository issues, reviews, discussions and public interactions representing this project.
